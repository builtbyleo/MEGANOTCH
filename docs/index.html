Install

  1. Download MegaNotch.zip and unzip it.
  2. Drag MegaNotch.app into Applications.
  3. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer. That's because an Apple Developer ID costs $99/year, and that's $99 that could go into notch.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Or, in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MegaNotch.app

To quit: click Exit notch (coward), press ⌘Q, or right-click the notch. You can't use the menu bar, because the menu bar is notch.
